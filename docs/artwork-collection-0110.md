# Collection and theme artwork, 0.11.0

Created 2026-10-25 with the built-in image generation tool, using this plugin's original `assets/garden/sunflower.png` as a style reference. It was a reference, not an edit target. No Forest or Focus artwork was used. Final PNGs are copied without changing their pixels or alpha. Original plants and ambience remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

Both atlases are 2172 × 724 RGBA with actual transparency. `assets/collection/milestones.png` has four horizontally spaced badge cells (543 × 724); CSS retains their proportions. `assets/collection/themes.png` has three square 724 × 724 cells. The badges are decorative milestone illustrations, not a botanical species diagram. Theme decorations stay outside the playable island; their ground projection never changes flower positions. The default theme reuses the existing hydrangea thumbnail for its selection card.

## Final prompt: milestones.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one original transparent game achievement badge atlas, four equally sized square cells in ONE HORIZONTAL ROW, 4:1 overall sprite layout. Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, not an edit target: match its cute hand-painted cartoon garden style, fine warm olive-brown outlines, softly painted highlights, chubby shapes, gentle earthy colors; no realism or glossy 3D. Each cell has a fully isolated centered small medallion with adequate transparent padding and nothing crossing cell boundaries. Left to right: (1) one little white daisy blossom with two green leaves on an ivory scalloped ceramic medallion, (2) a peach terracotta basket holding a modest bouquet of ten tiny flowers on a sage round medallion, (3) an eight-petal rainbow floral wreath of varied muted garden flowers around a tiny smiling seed on a cream medallion, (4) a miniature flourishing garden around a warm brass watering can on a honey-gold scalloped medallion. These are four milestone badges for first bloom, ten blooms, eight species, hundred blooms. All are equal optical size, easy to recognize at 64 pixels. NO lettering, numerals, labels, ribbon text, logos, watermark, background or checkerboard. Real alpha transparency. Keep complete silhouettes, subtle painterly detail, matching the supplied sunflower aesthetic.
```

The generated atlas used a 3:1 canvas rather than the requested 4:1. Its four separate badges fit the four proportional cells and are consumed without stretching or editing.
