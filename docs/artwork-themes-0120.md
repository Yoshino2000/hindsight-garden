# Theme furniture, 0.12.0

Created with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-25. Original swing and sunflower PNGs were style references, not edit targets. Three original transparent atlases are copied without pixel edits; CSS selects their three equal cells. No third-party artwork was copied. Each atlas depicts the main furniture, rear accessory and small side accessory. Ground shadows are inexpensive static SVG ellipses, clipped to the same island. Meadow retains the original swing, sign and watering can. Other themes replace them and update the whole ground, front faces, bed, water, fence and surrounding palette. The eight flower images retain their own colours.

Rain is 2164 × 727; dusk and blossom are 2172 × 724. All are RGBA with alpha range 0–255. The rain sprite aspect ratio follows its actual cell dimensions rather than stretching the image. The artwork remains an illustration rather than a full 3D object; the static shadows are a small visual approximation.
